FAQ

Common questions about the league

Is slo-pitch the same as softball?

Slo-pitch is a form of softball. The pitcher lobs the ball in a high arc rather than throwing it fast, which makes it easier to hit and a lot more fun for a mixed-ability group. If you have played softball you will pick it up in an inning.

When does the league play?

Sunday mornings from May through August. Games start between 9:30 am and 12:00 pm depending on the slate, usually two or three time slots per game day.

Where are games played?

Diamonds across Kitchener and Waterloo. Bridgeport Sportsfield #2 and #3 carry most of the schedule, with Wilson Park also used. The Venues page lists them all with directions and municipal field-condition links.

How do I join?

The league accepts full team registrations. Individual players looking for a team can still get in touch and be pointed toward teams with space. Contact the league.

What does it cost to play?

$110 per player for the season. That covers diamond permits, balls, and umpires for the year-end tournament. Payment details are confirmed with your captain at registration. See the fees page for detail.

Is it competitive?

It is a recreational co-ed league. Teams are split into A and B divisions for playoffs so that post-season games are played against similar competition.

What rules do you play by?

Play Slo-Pitch rules, with some league variations. Bat and ball specifications are set by Play Slo-Pitch; head protection is optional. See the Rules page.

What happens if it rains?

Check your municipality's field-conditions page first, linked from Venues. Rained-out games are rescheduled where a diamond and date can be found. The full rain-out policy is to be confirmed.

How do scores and standings get updated?

Captains report the final score after each game. Standings recalculate automatically. A score reported by both teams shows as confirmed; if the two reports disagree it is held until the convenor resolves it.

Why are some past seasons incomplete?

The league's previous website was shut down and only part of its data could be recovered. What survived is published on the Seasons page, marked as partial where it is.

Do I need to be registered with Play Slo-Pitch?

Yes. Every player must be a registered Play Slo-Pitch member before taking the field, and must appear on a registered roster. Membership includes the liability waiver and secondary sport accident insurance. Because this is a team-based registered league, a Base Member registration is enough unless you also play tournaments, which needs the All-Access Annual Pass ($17.50). See the Rules page for detail.
