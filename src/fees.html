Fees

What it costs to play

$110
per player, per season

What your fee covers

  • Diamond permits Booking the ball diamonds from the city for every game day
  • Balls Game balls for the season
  • Umpires Officials for the year-end tournament
  • Season length Roughly 12 games across the regular season, plus playoffs

Shirts are arranged by each team, not by the league.

How to pay

Fees are collected by your team captain, who pays the league for the team as a whole. If you are joining a team, speak to your captain first.

Captains and new teams: get in touch and we will confirm the payment method and the deadline for the coming season.

Ask about payment

Common questions

Is it per player or per team?

Per player. A full team of around 13 players works out to roughly $1,430 for the season, usually collected by the captain and paid to the league together.

What if I join partway through the season?

Get in touch. Late-season joins are handled case by case depending on how many games are left.

Do I pay the league or my captain?

Your captain. The league deals with one payment per team rather than with individual players.

What if a game is rained out?

Rained-out games are rescheduled where a diamond and a date can be found. Fees are not adjusted for weather.

Does the fee include a shirt?

No. Teams arrange their own shirts. Some teams have a sponsor who helps cover them, see the sponsors page.
