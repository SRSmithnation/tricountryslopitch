Players Wanted

Adult co-ed recreational slo-pitch, Sunday mornings from May through August, on diamonds across Kitchener and Waterloo. All skill levels are welcome.

WhenSunday mornings
SeasonMay to August
WhereKitchener & Waterloo
Format7 innings, co-ed

Joining

Tell us whether you are an individual looking for a team or a full team looking for a league. We will come back to you with the details, including the fee and the start date for the coming season.

What it is like

Seven innings, no hard time limit, usually about an hour and a half. The batting order alternates between men and women.

Competitive enough to be worth winning, relaxed enough that nobody is shouting at you.

On your own?

Most people come as part of a team, but if you are on your own we will try to place you with a team that is short on players.
