# Google Business Profile setup

Everything decided for the listing, so it can be filled in once and matched
across the other directories. Set up free at https://business.google.com

This is the biggest single lever for local search. It puts the league in the
map pack for searches like "slo pitch kitchener", which sits above the normal
results.

## Fields

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Business name | `Tri-County Slo-Pitch League` |
| Primary category | Softball club, if offered |
| Extra categories | Sports club, and any accurate amateur sports club or sports association option |
| Location type | Service-area business, **not** a storefront |
| Service area | Kitchener, Waterloo |
| Website | https://tricountyslopitch.ca |
| Hours | leave blank, see below |
| Phone | see below |

## Business name

Use the exact name, nothing appended. Not "Tri-County Slo-Pitch League -
Softball Kitchener Waterloo". Google requires the real-world name and keyword
stuffing is a common cause of profiles being suspended, which costs more than
the keywords gain. Location and category fields exist for that.

The name must be **identical everywhere**: this profile, the site title,
slopitch1.com, slopitchcentral.com and Facebook. Google cross-references them,
and a consistent name, area and contact is what establishes the league as real.

## Category

Most specific accurate option wins. "Sports club" could be curling or
pickleball, so it tells Google nothing. "Softball club" matches what people
actually search.

Only pick categories that are true. Adding "Baseball club" to catch more
searches risks a flag and dilutes the signal anyway.

The **primary** category matters most, and changing it later resets some local
ranking signals, so get it right at setup rather than switching in a month.

## Hours: leave blank

Setting Sunday 9am to 1pm means the profile displays **"Closed"** six days a
week and all winter. Someone finding the league in February sees something that
looks dead, which is the opposite of what a recruitment listing should do.

Google does not require hours. Without them the open/closed line simply does
not appear. The season and game times live in the description instead.

If hours are ever wanted, the least bad version is Sunday only, 9am to 1pm, with
Google's special hours marking the off-season closure. That is annual
maintenance that will get forgotten and then show wrong information.

## Phone

Do not use the personal mobile. It becomes public and permanent, and it is
exactly the problem that left Brad's contact details on the slopitch1.com
listing years after he stopped running the league.

Options:
- a free second number, for example TextNow, which gives Canadian numbers.
  Google Voice does not work in Canada
- pair it with the league email addresses in issue #57, so the next convenor
  inherits an address and a number rather than personal details

Verification for a service-area business is often done by **video** rather than
a phone call, so a number may not be needed to verify, only to display.

## Description

716 characters, inside Google's 750 limit.

```
Tri-County Slo-Pitch League is an adult co-ed recreational softball league in Kitchener and Waterloo, Ontario. We play slo-pitch rules on Sunday mornings from May through August, at Bridgeport and other local diamonds.

Games are seven innings with no hard time limit, usually about an hour and a half, and the batting order alternates between men and women. It is competitive enough to be worth winning and relaxed enough that nobody is shouting at you.

All skill levels are welcome. Most players join as part of a team, and if you are on your own we will try to place you with a team that is short of players. New teams are welcome too.

Schedules, standings, team pages and the current season are on our website.
```

Why it is written this way:

- the first sentence carries softball, slo-pitch, co-ed, adult, Kitchener and
  Waterloo naturally. Google weights the opening, and it still reads like a
  human wrote it
- the season and game times are here, which is why hours can stay blank
- **no URL.** Google discourages links in descriptions, they are not clickable,
  and the website field above does the job
- **no fee.** It invites the conversation rather than filtering people out at a
  glance, and a stale price on a Google profile is worse than none
- "nobody is shouting at you" is the line that sells it, and the honest
  difference from the commercial leagues

## Photos

Upload the flyer and the crest from `reference/flyers/`. Profiles with photos
get noticeably more engagement. Avoid photos with recognisable faces unless
those people have agreed.

## Realistic expectation

The league will rank for its own name quickly. Generic terms like "softball
league kitchener" are contested by LUG Sports and JAM Sports, who run paid ads
and have years of history. They also charge considerably more, so the audience
overlap is limited: people looking for a cheap local league are exactly the
ones who will choose this listing.
