Rules

How the league plays

Some league-specific rules are still being rebuilt. The league's previous website was shut down and its rules document was lost with it. The sections below are accurate; anything marked to be confirmed is being restored.

Governing rules

The Tri-County Slo-Pitch League is a member of Slo-Pitch Ontario. Play follows the SPO rulebook except where the league has agreed its own variations.

League format

  • Division Sunday Co-Ed
  • When Sunday mornings, May through August
  • Where Diamonds across Kitchener and Waterloo — see Venues
  • Teams 7 in the 2026 season
  • Season length 42 regular-season games across 10 game days
  • Game length 7 innings, no set time limit — games usually run about 1½ hours
  • Batting order Alternating, male then female, through the lineup
  • Playoffs Split into A and B divisions after the regular season, finals in late August

Player registration

All players must appear on an SPO Registered Roster. Everyone listed should read the release and indemnity form that accompanies the roster. Rosters are managed through the SPO registry.

Reporting scores

Both teams report the final score after each game. Where both reports agree, the result is marked confirmed. Where they disagree, the score is held back until the convenor settles it.
